# Niche Intelligence Evidence Ladder

Public facts and measurements may support descriptive observations. Observations may support unapproved hypotheses, and hypotheses may support proposed opportunities. None of these creates channel strategy, script, design, or Engine rules.

`MARKET_EVIDENCE`, `CREATIVE_REFERENCE`, and `VISUAL_GROUNDING` are separate roles. Market evidence is not eligible as Studio art or design authority. Channel-specific conclusions remain Channel memory; this generic method is available only when Engine memory is explicitly requested.
